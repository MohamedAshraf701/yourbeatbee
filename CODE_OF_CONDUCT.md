# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

Our Pledge

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming community, we as contributors
and maintainers pledge to make participation in our project and our community a
harassment-free experience for everyone.

Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination of any kind
- Abusive or derogatory comments
- Personal attacks

Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported by contacting the project maintainers at `security@yourbeatbee.dev`.
All complaints will be reviewed and investigated and will result in a response
that is deemed necessary and appropriate to the circumstances. The project
maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter
of any incident.

For full details, see: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
